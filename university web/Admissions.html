Admissions at Invertis University

We welcome students from across India and abroad to join our diverse academic community. Our admissions process is designed to be transparent, fair, and student-friendly.

Explore undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across Engineering, Management, Science, Arts, Law, and more.

📌 How to Apply

  1. Fill out the online application form.
  2. Upload required documents (marksheets, ID proof, etc.).
  3. Pay the application fee online.
  4. Appear for entrance exam/interview (if applicable).
  5. Receive admission confirmation and join orientation.
